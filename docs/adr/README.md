# Architecture Decision Records

ADRs record decisions that materially affect architecture. They explain why the current design exists rather than merely restating implementation.

## Statuses

- **Proposed:** being evaluated.
- **Accepted:** current decision.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a newer ADR.
- **Rejected:** considered but not adopted.

## Template

```md
# ADR-NNN: Title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem/constraints require a decision?

## Decision

What will we do?

## Alternatives considered

What credible alternatives were considered and why not chosen?

## Consequences

What benefits, costs, risks and follow-up work result?
```

ADRs should be updated by superseding decisions rather than rewriting history after implementation changes.
